Three pillars

One chain, three links

Modeled on the CA/Browser Forum: a governance root, a working group of intermediates, and individual senders at the leaf — each link only trusts the one directly above it.

Root — Governance

Regulatory & standards mandate

A neutral governance body sets issuance rules and liability terms, with FCC/FTC-style mandates giving carriers a reason to require the credential rather than merely accept it.

→
Intermediate — Working group

Industry cooperation

Carriers, mailbox providers, and messaging platforms co-develop the verification protocol and revocation infrastructure — the same room that made STIR/SHAKEN interoperable within the US.

→
Leaf — Sender

Cryptographic identity

Individual senders and organizations hold a signed, revocable credential checked in real time before delivery — the actual thing that stops spoofing at the last hop.

Positioning

Why existing standards stop short

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and STIR/SHAKEN each solved a real piece of this problem — but each stalls in a different place. Sender Chain is built to pick up exactly where they stop.
